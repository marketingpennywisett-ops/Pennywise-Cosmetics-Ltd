Pennywise opens 11th store: Pennywise Plaza, Trincity

Our newest location officially opened its doors in Trincity, creating more than 200 jobs in the area. CEO Dalvi Paladee reaffirmed our commitment to keeping prices competitive as customers navigate the rising cost of living, and to continuing to support locally made products on our shelves.

Pennywise named exclusive retailer of ZO Skin Health

Following a resolved legal dispute over the medical-grade skincare line, Pennywise reached an agreement with the brand's distributor and is now the nation's exclusive authorised commercial retailer of ZO Skin Health products, available at all our locations.

Building homes, one weekend at a time
